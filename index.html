會籍申請說明Notes on membership application
  1. 有關會籍申請的章則及會員之權利義務，均已清楚列於本會網頁（www.jnuhkaa.org），請仔細閱讀。The rules on membership and members' rights and obligations are set out on the Association's website (www.jnuhkaa.org). Please read them carefully.
  2. 會籍基本分為兩種：There are two basic types of membership:
    • 基本會員：凡曾經在暨南大學就讀、任教或任職的香港居民，認同本會宗旨，接受本會會章，及願意繳交本會訂立的入會費，均可申請加入為基本會員。Basic member: Hong Kong residents who have studied, taught or worked at Jinan University, who support the Association's aims, accept its constitution and are willing to pay the joining fee, may apply to be a basic member.
    • 學生會員：凡正在暨南大學就讀的香港學生，包括兼讀制，亦包括暨南大學在香港主辦或合辦課程，認同本會宗旨，接受本會會章，均可申請加入為學生會員。學生會員的會籍隨著該學生畢業或其他原因離校而終止。學生會員願意繳交本會訂立的入會費，可於任何時間申請轉為基本會員。Student member: Hong Kong students currently studying at Jinan University, including part-time students and those on programmes run or jointly run by Jinan University in Hong Kong, who support the Association's aims and accept its constitution, may apply to be a student member. Student membership ends when the student graduates or leaves the university for any other reason. A student member who is willing to pay the joining fee may apply to become a basic member at any time.
  3. 入會費及交費辦法：基本會員須繳納入會費港幣 100 元正；學生會員無需繳付入會費。付款方式見下方。Joining fee and how to pay: basic members pay a joining fee of HK$100; student members pay no joining fee. Payment methods are shown below.

個人資料收集聲明 Personal Information Collection Statement

暨南大學香港校友會有限公司（下稱「本會」）重視並致力保障你的個人資料私隱，並依據《個人資料（私隱）條例》（香港法例第486章，下稱「條例」）處理你的資料。請於提交前細閱本聲明。

一、資料的提供屬自願性質

標有「*」的欄位為處理入會申請所必需；如不提供，本會可能無法處理你的申請。其他欄位屬自願提供。

二、收集的資料類別

姓名、性別、出生日期、聯絡資料（電話、電郵、地址）、居住地區、在暨南大學的學歷及就讀資料、職業及行業資料；以及（如你選擇提供）香港身份證號碼、專業資格、社會公職、學生證／畢業證書等證明文件、付款證明，和你就校友活動興趣、參與角色、導師意願及建議所作的回應。

三、收集及使用目的

(1) 審批及管理會籍（包括核實資格、處理會費、編配會員編號）；
(2) 提供會員服務及必要的行政通訊（如會籍確認、續費及資料變更通知）；
(3) 在你同意的前提下，向你發送本會會訊、活動及聯誼資訊（見第六項「直接促銷」）；
(4) 策劃及改善校友活動、地區及學院聯絡網絡、導師計劃及相關服務，包括作統計及分析（結果不會識別個別人士）；
(5) 如你表示願意擔任導師、地區／學院聯絡人、義工或分享嘉賓，或願意接收／提供內推及招聘資訊，本會會就相關事宜聯絡你；
(6) 履行法律或監管規定。

本會不會將你的資料用於上述以外的用途，除非獲你同意或法律許可。

四、資料的披露及轉移

本會不會出售你的資料，亦不會在未獲你另行同意前，向其他會員、僱主或任何第三方披露你的聯絡資料（例如導師配對，本會會先與你確認）。本會只會向以下人士披露或轉移資料：(a) 代本會提供網站、資料儲存、電郵及資訊科技服務的服務供應商（可能位於香港以外地區），該等供應商只可按本會指示處理資料，並須採取適當保安措施；(b) 法律、法院或政府／監管機構要求披露的情況。

五、資料保安及保存

你的資料存放於本會指定、設有存取控制的系統，只有獲授權人員可查閱。未獲批准或未完成的申請資料，本會會於 12 個月內刪除；會員資料會於會籍有效期內保存，並於會籍終止後最多再保存 2 年（用作處理查詢、重新入會及履行法律義務），其後刪除或匿名化。

六、直接促銷（只在你於下方選擇同意時適用）

如你選擇同意，本會擬使用你的姓名及聯絡資料（電郵、電話、WhatsApp／微信、郵寄地址），透過上述途徑向你發送：(i) 本會會訊及活動邀請；(ii) 會員服務及校友福利資訊；(iii) 本會的內推／招聘資訊（如你已表示願意）。本會在未獲你同意前不會作此使用，亦不會把你的資料提供予第三方作其促銷用途。你可隨時免費要求本會停止使用你的資料作直接促銷（見第七項聯絡方式），本會會即時停止，不收取任何費用。

七、你的權利及聯絡方式

根據條例，你有權：(1) 查詢本會是否持有你的個人資料及索取副本（本會可能就處理查閱要求收取合理的費用）；(2) 要求更正不準確的資料；(3) 隨時撤回同意，或要求停止接收通訊。請以書面（包括電郵）向本會提出。

八、資料準確性

申請人確認所提供的資料出於自願，且屬實及完整；資料如有更改，將主動通知本會。如申請人未滿 18 歲，須由家長或監護人閱讀並同意本聲明。

English version (the Chinese text and English text are intended to have the same meaning).

Jinan University Hong Kong Alumni Association Limited (the “Association”) is committed to protecting your personal data privacy and handles your data in accordance with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) (the “Ordinance”). Please read this statement before you submit.

1. Supply of data is voluntary. Fields marked “*” are necessary to process your application; if you do not provide them we may be unable to process it. All other fields are optional.

2. Kinds of data collected. Name, gender, date of birth, contact details (phone, email, address), district of residence, study details at Jinan University, occupation and industry; and, if you choose to provide them, Hong Kong ID number, professional qualifications, public service, supporting documents (student card / graduation certificate), proof of payment, and your answers about activity interests, roles, mentoring willingness and comments.

3. Purposes. (1) assessing and administering membership (eligibility checks, fees, membership numbers); (2) providing member services and necessary administrative notices (membership confirmation, renewal, changes); (3) with your consent, sending the Association’s newsletters, event and social information (see section 6); (4) planning and improving alumni activities, district and college networks, the mentoring programme and related services, including statistics and analysis that do not identify individuals; (5) if you indicate willingness to act as a mentor, district/college liaison, volunteer or guest speaker, or to receive/offer referral and job information, contacting you about those matters; (6) complying with legal or regulatory requirements. We will not use your data for other purposes without your consent or legal authority.

4. Disclosure and transfer. We do not sell your data and will not disclose your contact details to other members, employers or any third party without your separate consent (for example, we will check with you before any mentor matching). We may disclose or transfer data only to (a) service providers who provide website, data storage, email and IT services for the Association (who may be located outside Hong Kong), and who may process data only on our instructions and must apply appropriate security measures; and (b) where required by law, a court, or a government/regulatory authority.

5. Security and retention. Your data is kept in access-controlled systems available only to authorised persons. Data of applications that are not approved or not completed is deleted within 12 months; member data is kept while membership is in force and for up to 2 years after membership ends (to handle enquiries, re-joining and legal obligations), then deleted or anonymised.

6. Direct marketing (applies only if you consent below). If you consent, we intend to use your name and contact details (email, phone, WhatsApp/WeChat, postal address) to send you through those channels: (i) the Association’s newsletters and event invitations; (ii) information on member services and alumni benefits; (iii) the Association’s referral / job information (if you have said you are willing). We will not do so without your consent, and we will not provide your data to third parties for their own marketing. You may ask us at any time, free of charge, to stop using your data for direct marketing (contact details in section 7); we will stop without charge.

7. Your rights and contact. Under the Ordinance you may (1) ask whether we hold your personal data and request a copy (we may charge a reasonable fee for processing access requests); (2) request correction of inaccurate data; (3) withdraw consent or ask to stop receiving communications at any time. Please make requests in writing (including by email).

8. Accuracy. The applicant confirms the data provided is voluntary, true and complete and will notify the Association of changes. If the applicant is under 18, a parent or guardian must read and agree to this statement.
